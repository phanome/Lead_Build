📐 Segmentation Logic

🥇 Tier 1 — High Priority
Strong buying signal (funded, competitor use, or legacy system) + target company size. These get personalized outreach within 24h.
🥈 Tier 2 — Warm Prospects
Moderate signal or adjacent industry fit. Add to an automated nurture sequence and revisit each quarter for updated triggers.
🥉 Tier 3 — Watch List
No current buying signal or poor size fit. Keep in CRM, set a 90-day check-in reminder. A trigger event can fast-track to Tier 1.
🔍 Signal Sources
LinkedIn job posts (hiring), BuiltWith (tech stack), LinkedIn Sales Navigator (funding alerts), G2 reviews (competitor pain points).
